# Goal

A Personal Financial Advisor. Investment focused Agent. 

# Technical

## APIs

\- Personal saving accounts and investments accounts. This could be via individual institutions APIs or unified APIs like plaid or copilot. For the prototype it's perfectly fine to access my accounts data as a data dump, and later develop automatic real time links.  
\- real time and historical stock market, real estate and other investments data  
\- News sources

## UX

\- Ideally a chatbot, like OpenClaw (https://openclaw.ai/)  
\- needs the ability to display data, charts, long arguments, comparison, ... Those could be displayed in separate links outside the interaction challenge. Example it's fine to interact with the agent via chat, and it points to a web UI (laptop and mobile friendly) for further details

# Investments Capabilities

\- It is highly customizable for different styles for investments, different time horizons, and any arbitrary rule. Examples:   
\- \- buy and hold strategy  
\- \- ethical investments: no investment in military companies, or sustainable  
\- \- Any arbitrary personal rule  
\- \- time windows: 6 months, 2 years, 10+ years, ...  
\- Investment scope: 

* V1: Stock market,   
* V2: Real Estate  
* V3: Private Market

Investment amount: $500k \- $1m  
Agents won’t need the ability to make any trades in the first couple versions. 

## Out of scope

- Crypto  
- Day trading
